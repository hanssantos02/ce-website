# CEPath Strategy

CEPath is a working title. This document records the Product Owner's
brief; proposed metrics and tracks remain subject to review.

## Purpose

Filipino civil engineering students preparing for the CELE need to
understand why engineering formulas and solutions work, while building
foundations and organizing their review across a broad curriculum.
Memorizing answers alone does not meet that learning need.

## Positioning

Provide an original, structured learning path that connects concepts,
engineering explanations, and practice before tracking completion.
Recommend a clear sequence while keeping every published free lesson
open to anonymous learners regardless of prerequisites or completion history.
Prioritize a small, academically reliable learning experience over
rapid expansion of the lesson catalog.

## Users

**Primary:** Civil engineering students preparing for the Philippine
CELE — use CEPath to study in a recommended sequence, understand
engineering reasoning, practice problems, and resume their review.

**Also served:** Students building foundations, graduating students,
and retakers; differences in their needs remain to be validated.

## Boundaries

- Philippine CELE preparation is the initial scope.
- No payments, subscriptions, artificial premium locks, or fictional pricing.
- No PRC affiliation, invented official questions, or passing guarantees.
- No bulk placeholder curriculum or premature CMS and service complexity.
- Work on one approved, independently testable milestone at a time.
- No mandatory lesson unlocking or prerequisite/completion-based access barriers;
  curriculum guidance remains advisory (CE-NAV-001).

## Key metrics

Proposed for Product Owner review; no measurement system or targets exist.
Lesson completion is an engagement signal, not proof of mastery.

- **First lesson completion rate** — share of learners starting a lesson
  who complete it; future lesson/progress records.
- **Practice learning gain** — change on comparable exercises before
  and after study; future reviewed assessments.
- **Return-to-study rate** — share of learners who resume within seven
  days of a study session; future progress records.
- **Concept clarity feedback** — learner reports of whether an explanation
  helped them understand why a solution works; future pilot feedback.

## Tracks

Proposed investment areas drawn from the brief, not implementation approval.

### Curriculum reliability

Maintain reviewed explanations, calculations, units, prerequisites,
and sources.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Understanding depends on trustworthy content.

### Structured study experience

Make the subject → module → lesson sequence readable and accessible,
with recommended order and unrestricted access to published free lessons.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Learners need a clear path from concept to practice.

### Learning continuity

Support meaningful completion and reliable account-backed resumption,
including out-of-order study and separate continue/recommended-next actions.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Learners need to continue their study across sessions.

## Brand

**One-liner:** A structured civil engineering learning platform for
Filipino students preparing for the CELE.

**Key message:** Learn the concept. Understand the engineering.
Practice the problem. Track your progress.
